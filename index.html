03

Multi-Agent Ransomware Detector in progress

Windows · Sysmon · MITRE ATT&CK · research with two co-authors

Most detectors answer one question: threat or no threat. This architecture runs on the host, with one agent per log source reading Sysmon telemetry, and an orchestrator that correlates their alerts to reconstruct the attack sequence in MITRE ATT&CK terms before recommending a mitigation. It is evaluated against a monolithic classifier in Windows 11 virtual machines on anticipation, traceability and false-positive rate.

  • Sysmon
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • Multi-agent
  • Forensics
  • Windows 11
